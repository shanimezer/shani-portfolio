The short version

Story first.
Medium second.

My work moves between film, animation, games, social media and interactive experiences. I am drawn to projects that build worlds, shape communities and make an audience feel like they have stepped inside something rather than simply watched it.

I combine cinematic thinking with production structure and a hands-on understanding of digital tools. Depending on the project, that can mean directing performers, shaping a game narrative, designing cinematics, running a production pipeline, creating content for social platforms or experimenting with new AI-assisted workflows.

The medium changes. The goal stays the same: make the idea feel alive.
